What We Do

Two7 Investments is Emory's stock pitch competition club. We train members to develop institutional-quality investment theses and compete at the highest levels nationally.

Stock Pitch Excellence

We focus on one thing: crafting winning stock pitches. No fluff, no filler—just rigorous fundamental analysis and the skills to present conviction-level investment ideas.

Open to All

Every Emory student is welcome. Start by shadowing the pod of your choice, learn from experienced members, and work through our training pipeline to join a competition team.

Compete Nationally

We send teams to premier stock pitch competitions across the country. Our members go head-to-head with top programs and consistently deliver.

Real Skills, Real Results

Learn financial modeling, valuation techniques, and how to construct a compelling investment narrative. These skills translate directly to careers in finance.
